## 1. Executive Summary

This inventory audits the proposed source datasets for the 5.00-Billion-token pretraining mixture for ModernGPT-S 500M. Each candidate source is evaluated for legal licensing compatibility, provenance, document volume, raw storage footprint, and data quality risks.

### Legal & Licensing Policy:
- **Commercial & Permissive Compliance:** Permitted licenses include Apache 2.0, MIT, BSD, ODC-By 1.0, CC0, CC-BY 4.0, and Public Domain.
- **Copyleft & Restrictive Exclusions:** CC-BY-NC (Non-Commercial), CC-BY-ND (No Derivatives), and ambiguous scraping dumps with copyrighted literary works under active copyright (e.g. Books3, BookCorpus) are strictly **EXCLUDED**.
- **Public Domain Only for Prose:** All literary prose must originate from verified public-domain sources (Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks).

## 3. Domain-Specific Quality & Risk Analysis

### 3.1 Quality Web Text (FineWeb-Edu)
- **Quality Score:** High. FineWeb-Edu filters Common Crawl with an educational-value classifier trained on synthetic quality annotations, filtering out SEO spam, affiliate links, and toxic material.
- **Risks:** Machine-generated boilerplate, template navigation headers, cookie consent banners.
- **Mitigation:** Regex header/footer stripping and MinHash deduplication.

### 3.2 Educational Material (Cosmopedia v2)
- **Quality Score:** Exceptionally high factual density and clean synthetic textbook exposition generated by Mixtral-8x22B.
- **Risks:** Syntactic uniformity, predictable introductory phrases ("In this chapter, we will explore...").
- **Mitigation:** Down-sample repetitive introductory headers; cap domain proportion at $15\%$ of total mixture.

### 3.3 Source Code (The Stack v2 Permissive Subset)
- **Quality Score:** High utility for programming syntax, reasoning, and tool use.
- **Risks:** Autogenerated files (e.g. `package-lock.json`, minified JS, protobuf outputs), vendored dependencies, duplicate libraries, exposed API keys/secrets.
- **Mitigation:** Filter files by maximum line length ($<1000$ chars), alphanumeric ratio ($>0.6$), file size ($1\text{ KB} - 100\text{ KB}$), and regex token scanners for credentials. Exclude `.min.js`, lockfiles, and compiler outputs.

### 3.4 Books & Prose (Public Domain Only)
- **Licensing Audit:** Books3 and raw BookCorpus datasets contain copyrighted works published after 1928, exposing training pipelines to legal risk and DCMA liability.
- **Decision:** Restrict prose to **Public Domain only** (pre-1929 publications or explicit CC0 releases via Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks).
- **Risks:** Archaic language patterns (19th-century syntax).
- **Mitigation:** Balance with modern web prose and cap book share at $10\%$ ($500\text{M tokens}$).

### 3.5 Curated Dialogue (UltraChat 200k / OpenAssistant)
- **Quality Score:** High conversational naturalness.
- **Risks:** Risk of contaminating pretraining with SFT instruction patterns or evaluation test sets.
- **Mitigation:** Quarantined multi-turn conversational exchanges only; eliminate benchmark contamination strings (GSM8k, MMLU, HumanEval) before ingestion.
